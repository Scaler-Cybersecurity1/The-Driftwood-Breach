    The Driftwood Breach

    You're a Tier-2 analyst at Driftwood Analytics, a SaaS company. A customer-success manager reports a phishing email, and minutes of odd traffic follow on the customer portal. Work out what happened — by searching the logs yourself.

    Your roleTier-2 SOC analyst
    Time needed25–35 minutes
    You willDissect 1 email · search the logs · make 2 calls

    How this lab works

    1. There are no multiple-choice answers to find facts. You read the reported email and search the access logs in the console on the right to work the answers out yourself.
    2. Each step unlocks the next. Type what the evidence actually shows — an account name, an IP address, a domain — into the answer boxes.
    3. The logs hold hundreds of lines of normal traffic and decoys. Use the search box and quick filters to narrow them down, the way you would in a real SOC.
    4. Two response decisions at the end set your grade. The flag is built only from the facts you recover. Submit it on your course platform.
    5. Nothing leaves your browser; answers are checked locally. Progress is saved on this device.
